## About Us
**Mission statement** — S+ Dental aims to increase the quality of life for our community by providing
top-notch customized dental services for our patients. Furthermore, we expect everyone who finds value in
our mission to be the oral health ambassadors themselves to help improve others' quality of life by sharing
dental knowledge gained from visits with us.

S+ Dental opened in June 2023. Many have wondered what S+ means. It came from the game rank system where it
acknowledges superb performance. As an avid gamer, Dr. Stephanie Kang always strives to be the S rank player
in the gaming world and relates mastery in real life, thus S+ dentist.

With a vision that everyone who encounters S+ Dental will experience something special that's above and
beyond what they've experienced at dental visits, Dr. Kang began the journey of start-up practice in general
dentistry. Our focus is on increasing quality of life for patients by improving oral health. We'll guide
patients to achieve what they need and want for a healthy smile. Ultimately, we want our community to enjoy
more quality time with family and friends instead of worrying about dental problems. Come meet us and let
us support your S+ journey to optimal health!

**Dr. Stephanie Kang** obtained a Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Tufts University School of Dental
Medicine in 2012 in Boston, Massachusetts. After completing a year of AEGD (Advanced Education in General
Dentistry) residency program through Lutheran Medical Center (now called NYU Langone Health), Dr. Kang
worked in a private dental practice in the Greater Boston area. After visiting Seattle in summer time,
Dr. Kang fell in love with the city and moved to Seattle in 2015. Since then, Dr. Kang had an opportunity to
work at various community health centers and tribal clinics in Washington state, providing quality of care
to the underserved population. As Dr. Kang enjoys building relationships with patients and coworkers, she
has signed up for locum opportunities at many distant locations including Barrow in Alaska and La Push in
Washington state. By providing dental care to various communities, Dr. Kang has gained knowledge and
expertise as a primary dental provider.

"Outside of dentistry, I enjoy spending time with family and friends, playing games, drinking boba tea
(with 30% sugar or less), watching K-dramas and challenging myself with Chopin etudes piano music."

## New Patients → Your First Visit
Here at S+ Dental, we believe in providing a very thorough service in order to achieve a healthy oral
health. During your first visit, we will perform a Comprehensive Exam, which takes about 1-1.5 hours. In
order to do that, a set of radiographs are needed. If you have recent radiographs from another office,
please either contact them to forward it to us or provide us the information at least one week prior to the
appointment. You may need to sign a dental records release form. After your exam, we will create a plan
tailored to your oral health needs including the type of dental cleaning. Based on your needs, you may get
a cleaning the same day or you may need to return for additional visit. Please provide your insurance
information prior to your appointment in order for us to verify your coverage for the visit. Please arrive
at least 15 minutes prior to your appointments to fill out any necessary paperwork.
