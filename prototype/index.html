About us

Why “S+”?

In game ranking systems, S+ is the top tier — reserved for truly superb performance. Dr. Kang, an avid gamer, brings that same standard to dentistry: every visit should feel above and beyond what you've experienced before.

S+ Dental opened in June 2023 with one mission: improve our community's quality of life by improving oral health — so you can spend more time with family and friends and less time worrying about your teeth.

“Outside of dentistry, I enjoy spending time with family and friends, playing games, drinking boba tea (30% sugar or less), watching K-dramas and challenging myself with Chopin études.” — Dr. Kang
Meet the dentist

Dr. Stephanie Kang, DMD

Dr. Kang fell in love with Seattle on a summer visit and moved here in 2015. Since then she has cared for patients at community health centers and tribal clinics across Washington, and taken locum posts as far as Utqiaġvik (Barrow), Alaska and La Push, WA.

  • Doctor of Dental Medicine, Tufts University School of Dental Medicine (2012)
  • AEGD residency, Lutheran Medical Center (now NYU Langone Health)
  • Private practice in Greater Boston before moving to Washington
  • Years of primary dental care for underserved communities across WA
